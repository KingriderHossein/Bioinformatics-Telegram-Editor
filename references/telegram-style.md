# Telegram Style

## Language

Write natural Persian. Keep precision-sensitive English names and identifiers unchanged.

## Readability

- Use short paragraphs, normally 1-3 sentences.
- Put the strongest verified fact near the top.
- Explain unavoidable technical terms once in simple language.
- Avoid academic filler and long literature-review introductions.
- Avoid repetitive headings inside a short post.

## Formatting

- Use 1-4 purposeful emojis per post, not an emoji on every line.
- Use emphasis sparingly.
- Use 2-4 relevant hashtags at most when hashtags are useful.
- Keep the source identification at the end.
- Do not stuff the post with raw citations or multiple long links.

## Scientific numbers

Preserve exact values that carry the story. Keep units and version strings unambiguous. Do not change a relative improvement into an absolute improvement or vice versa.
