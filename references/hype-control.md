# Hype and Fact-Check Gate

Run this gate after drafting and before returning a post.

## Evidence-status gate

- Preprint: visibly state not peer reviewed.
- Author-reported benchmark: use qualified attribution.
- Independent challenge: distinguish challenge validation from broad real-world generalization.
- Database/software announcement: do not imply biological discovery.

## Study-design gate

- In vitro or cell data: do not imply human therapeutic efficacy.
- Animal data: do not imply proven human effects.
- Observational data: do not imply causation.
- Technical model performance: do not imply clinical readiness.
- Small or narrow benchmark: do not generalize to the whole field.

## AI wording gate

Avoid unsupported anthropomorphic or capability claims such as:

- "AI understands biology"
- "AI discovered the cure"
- "AI can now replace experts"

Prefer concrete statements about what the model predicted, ranked, generated, classified, or optimized.

## Number gate

For every number in the draft, confirm:

- the number exists in the evidence
- the unit is preserved
- the comparator is preserved
- the population/dataset context is preserved
- percentage and percentage-point changes are not confused

## Final red-team question

Ask: "Could a careful reader interpret this sentence as stronger than the source evidence?" If yes, rewrite it.
