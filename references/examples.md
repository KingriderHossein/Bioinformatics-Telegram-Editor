# Editorial Examples

These examples show transformation logic, not reusable facts.

## Example: author-reported benchmark

Bad headline concept:
"New tool is 100 times faster than every competitor"

Better headline concept:
"A new tool reports a more than 100-fold speedup in its own benchmark"

Reason: preserve attribution and benchmark scope.

## Example: preprint

Bad body concept:
"Researchers proved that the model detects enzyme function incorrectly."

Better body concept:
"In a new preprint that has not yet been peer reviewed, the authors report that several models can remain confident even after key active-site features are disrupted."

Reason: preserve publication status and avoid overgeneralization.

## Example: infrastructure change

Bad opening:
"NCBI released an update."

Better opening concept:
"If your pipeline downloads this resource through the legacy endpoint, this update may break it."

Reason: lead with user impact.
